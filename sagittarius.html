Why open-weight matters here

Works on the ridge

The model runs through Ollama on your laptop. No signal at the trailhead means no problem; the weather lookup is the only online part and is optional.

Your places stay yours

Where you hike, bird, or garden is personal. Prompts go to localhost, not to a server you don't control.

Swap the brain

The model menu lists whatever you've pulled: Gemma, Llama, Qwen. Change the system prompt or fine-tune on your regional bird list without asking anyone.
Sagittarius is one HTML file. Weather by Open-Meteo (free, keyless). Language model: any open-weight model served by Ollama.
